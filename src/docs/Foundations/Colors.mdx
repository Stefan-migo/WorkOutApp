## Accent discipline (DSF-1c)

- At most **two visible accent uses per screen** — links count.
- Accent is never a page wash (the page canvas is `--color-bg`, always).
- Every accent **fill** carries `--color-accent-on` text.
- **No second accent**: no blue, purple, indigo, or violet anywhere in the app.
